01 / INTRODUCTION

Abstract.

Safe navigation among pedestrians requires robots to respond to diverse interactions while maintaining efficient progress toward their goals. RANGER investigates a unified framework for learning multiple social navigation behaviors from interaction-rich demonstrations and guided reinforcement post-training. A multi-expert demonstration pipeline supplies varied interactions, while differentiable social objectives guide stochastic policy rollouts toward promising behaviors. Group-relative updates then reinforce these outcomes in the learned policy. The resulting framework is designed to support adaptable, interaction-aware robot motion across challenging social navigation situations.
